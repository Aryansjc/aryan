Constraints are a design education. In a terminal you get 80 columns, 25 rows, and a palette someone else chose in 1978. Every layout decision becomes honest.

Building vsh taught me more about hierarchy, spacing and feedback than any visual tool — because when you can't decorate, you have to **structure**.

If you want to get better at interface design, spend a week designing for the worst renderer you own.

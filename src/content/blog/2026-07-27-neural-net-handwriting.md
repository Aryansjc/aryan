Cortex started as a dare: build a neural network with nothing but NumPy. No PyTorch, no safety net. Week one was humbling — my "tiny" net had more bugs than weights.

The turning point was visualising the gradients. Once I could **see** the loss surface instead of printing a number, backprop stopped being a formula and became a direction.

Status: 94% on my own digits, 61% on my friend's. Apparently my handwriting is the easiest dataset in ML history. Next: augmentation, and a thicker skin.
